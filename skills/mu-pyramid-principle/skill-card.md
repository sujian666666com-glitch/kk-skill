## Description:

Pyramid Principle logic coaching skill that diagnoses writing structure, checks MECE and SCQA patterns, and rebuilds drafts into structured Markdown.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[muippt](https://clawhub.ai/user/muippt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Employees and external users use this skill to diagnose and restructure reports, proposals, speeches, emails, meeting notes, and presentation outlines using Pyramid Principle, MECE, and SCQA patterns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-provided reports, proposals, or drafts may contain sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Use only in agent environments approved for that content and rely on the agent/model privacy settings; the skill itself adds no storage, networking, or credential handling.

Risk: Restructuring can change emphasis or make unsupported claims appear more complete than the original evidence supports.

Mitigation: Review the diagnosis and optimized draft against the original source text before sharing, especially for business or external communication.
